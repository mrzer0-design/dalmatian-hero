About Mr. Zer0 Toys

Made in a barn, tested by a Dalmatian

Mr. Zer0 Toys started with one very spotty puppy and a pile of chewed-up slippers. We wanted toys that could keep up with him, so we started making our own from natural rubber and thick cotton rope.

Every toy is tested in the barn by Mr. Zer0 before it goes on sale. If it doesn't survive an afternoon of tugging, fetching and gnawing, it doesn't make the cut.
